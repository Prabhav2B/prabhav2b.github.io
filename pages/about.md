Hey, I'm *Prabhav Bhatnagar*! I am a game designer and developer from India, currently based in Finland. I am currently a [doctoral researcher exploring game feel](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AmMe8_EAAAAJ&hl=en) at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en/people/prabhav-bhatnagar), from where I previously completed a Master's in Game Design and Production. While research is my day job, I'm still actively involved with game development, even pushing for more [research-through-game-design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12887) within academia.

I find it fun to design games of various genres and scopes, and I am particularly enjoying puzzle design at the moment. I've also been exploring, learning and playing with shader development and technical-art.

Outside of game design, I like to play guitar and make music when possible. I have been [really getting into board game](https://boardgamegeek.com/collection/user/prabby_patty) for the past couple years and would love to design more board, card and paper games. I have been yo-yo-ing recently and that's been amazing haha. I have been geeking out on anything and everything sci-fi for some unknown reason and I'm loving it, if you have reccomendations hit me up!
