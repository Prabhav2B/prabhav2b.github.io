## Summary

*Aether-07* is a portal-inpired 3D first-person puzzle game. Set in a science-fiction setting, the player takes part in simulated tests aboard the spaceship Aether-07. Space and Time are at your fingers as you solve various physics puzzles and uncover the mysteries of the spaceship.

**My roles**: Solo-dev, key skill highlights -- *Game Design, Level Design, Programming and Technical Art*.
